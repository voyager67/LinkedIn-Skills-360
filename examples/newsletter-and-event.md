# Example: Newsletter and event

**Audience:** Professional creator / community lead

**Prompt to try**

> Use `linkedin-newsletter-strategy` and `linkedin-event-strategy`. Create a monthly newsletter for early-career developers and plan a 45-minute online session about project portfolio reviews. Define the audience promise, issue themes, speaker questions, moderation checklist and post-event follow-up. Avoid claiming attendance or subscriber growth.

**Good output:** clear editorial promise, sustainable cadence, event agenda, consent and follow-up plan.
