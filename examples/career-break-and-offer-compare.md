# Example: Career returner / offer decision

**Audience:** Career returner / experienced professional

**Prompt to try**

> Use `career-break-returner-positioning`, `skills-gap-and-learning-plan` and `offer-comparison-worksheet`. I am returning after a career break and have two offers. Help me describe my current skills without sharing private details about the break, compare the written terms against my priorities, and identify what needs clarification. Do not make the decision for me.

**Good output:** chosen disclosure level, current evidence map, transparent offer comparison and unanswered questions.
