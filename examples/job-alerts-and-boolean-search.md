# Example: Job alerts and Boolean search

**Audience:** Student / job seeker

**Prompt to try**

> Use `boolean-job-search-builder` and `job-alert-search-setup-planner`. I am a second-year IT student looking for backend or full-stack internships in Mumbai or remote India. Build separate broad and focused search strings, explain what each term does, and suggest saved-search filters and an alert cadence I can manage. Do not assume every Boolean operator works in every LinkedIn search surface.

**Good output:** distinct query variants, named alerts, syntax caveats and a practical review plan.
