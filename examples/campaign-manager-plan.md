# Example: Campaign Manager plan

**Audience:** Paid marketer / demand generation

**Prompt to try**

> Use `linkedin-ads-objective-and-budget-planner`, `linkedin-audience-targeting-review` and `linkedin-lead-gen-form-designer`. We are promoting a B2B webinar for supply-chain operations leaders. Recommend a campaign objective, audience filters, a minimal Lead Gen Form and a measurement plan. Mark budget and conversion assumptions clearly; do not claim to launch the campaign.

**Good output:** objective-to-outcome fit, safe audience criteria, minimal form fields, privacy review and reproducible measurement definitions.
