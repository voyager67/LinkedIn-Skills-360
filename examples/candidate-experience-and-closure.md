# Example: Candidate experience and closure

**Audience:** Recruiter / talent operations

**Prompt to try**

> Use `candidate-experience-journey`, `recruiter-communication-calendar` and `candidate-rejection-and-closure`. Review this anonymized hiring process. Identify where candidates lack updates, propose a communication calendar, and draft a respectful rejection that uses only the confirmed reason we are allowed to share.

**Good output:** a candidate journey, owners and timing, factual message drafts and privacy safeguards.
