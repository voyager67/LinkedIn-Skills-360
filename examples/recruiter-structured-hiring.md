# Example: Recruiter structured hiring

**Audience:** Recruiter / hiring manager

**Prompt to try**

> Use `recruiter-role-intake`, `competency-framework-designer`, `interview-plan-and-panel-designer` and `work-sample-assessment-designer`. We are hiring a junior data analyst. Define role outcomes, separate essential criteria from preferences, draft a three-stage interview loop, and design a short work sample using synthetic data. The panel must make all decisions; do not rank candidates.

**Good output:** approved-role questions, observable competencies, consistent questions, time-bounded work sample and human decision points.
