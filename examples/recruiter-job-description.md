# Example: Recruiter job description

**Audience:** Recruiter / hiring manager

**Prompt to try**

> Use `job-description-writer`. We need a junior customer support analyst. The role includes investigating customer issues, documenting recurring patterns, communicating with product teams, and maintaining help content. Draft a clear job description, separate essential requirements from preferences, and identify decisions still missing (location, compensation, shift expectations, and interview process). Do not add degree requirements unless supported by the work.
