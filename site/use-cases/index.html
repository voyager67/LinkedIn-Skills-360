LIBRARY / USE CASES
03 / BY AUDIENCE

Start with the
job to be done.

Different people use LinkedIn for different reasons. These paths group relevant workflows so you can get to a useful starting point without searching the entire catalogue.

PATH 01STUDENT / EARLY CAREER

Build a credible start.

Turn coursework, projects and real experience into a professional story. Find internships, reach out to alumni and prepare for early-career interviews without inflating your experience.

  • Student profile and project portfolio
  • Internship search and campus placement prep
  • Alumni networking and informational interviews
  • Interview stories and skill-gap planning

PATH 02JOB SEEKER / PROFESSIONAL

Make the search systematic.

Move from broad browsing to a clear search plan. Compare role requirements with your evidence, tailor materials truthfully and keep applications moving.

  • Profile audit and headline/About refinement
  • Boolean searches, job alerts and role-fit review
  • Resume evidence mapping and application tracking
  • Recruiter replies, interviews and offer comparison

PATH 03WORKING PROFESSIONAL

Build momentum over time.

Strengthen your reputation, maintain meaningful professional relationships and plan the next step inside or outside your current organization.

  • Thought leadership and professional content
  • Mentoring and relationship maps
  • Promotion cases and internal mobility
  • Career development and first-90-day planning

PATH 04CREATOR / FOUNDER

Publish with a point of view.

Create a coherent voice across your profile, posts, newsletters, events and company presence. Prioritize useful content over engagement tricks.

  • Content calendars and post writing
  • Newsletters, articles and events
  • Founder positioning and company-page planning
  • Community responses and employee advocacy

Turn research into relevance.

Prepare for the right account or conversation with public-source research, a thoughtful discovery plan and useful follow-up.

  • Sales Navigator search planning
  • Account intelligence and lead qualification

Make hiring more consistent.

Clarify the role, design a sourcing plan, communicate respectfully and keep assessment tied to observable job requirements.

  • Role intake and talent-market mapping

PATH 07HIRING MANAGER

Assess the work, not the persona.

Build a structured process around the actual job: competencies, interview questions, work samples, consistent evidence and accountable debriefs.

  • Competency frameworks and role alignment
  • Structured interview plans and scorecards
  • Work-sample design and panel calibration
  • Fairness, privacy and hiring debrief reviews

Connect objectives, audience, creative, lead capture and conversion measurement before spending budget.
