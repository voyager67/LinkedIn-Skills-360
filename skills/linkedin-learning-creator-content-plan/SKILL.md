# LinkedIn Learning Creator Content Plan

**Specialist:** Career Growth Coach  
**Category:** Marketing and learning

## When to use this skill

Use this workflow when: an instructor or subject expert wants to plan course or educational content for LinkedIn Learning Do not activate it merely because a related keyword appears in unrelated text.

## Inputs to collect

Collect instructor expertise, learner level, learning outcomes, course format, evidence, production capacity, licensing and editorial requirements.

Ask only for missing information that materially changes the recommendation. State assumptions when a first-pass answer can be made without blocking.

## Workflow

1. Define measurable learning outcomes and prerequisites
2. Break the topic into modules and practice checkpoints
3. Identify demonstrations, assessments and supporting assets
4. Check rights for all examples, code, media and third-party materials
5. Create a production and update plan

## Output format

Return the following sections, adapting them to the task rather than forcing empty sections:

- Course concept and audience
- Learning outcomes and outline
- Practice and assessment plan
- Rights, review and maintenance checklist

## Example prompt

> Create a beginner course outline about practical Excel analysis for early-career professionals. Flag assets or examples that need licensing review.

## Edge cases and failure handling

- Creator eligibility, commissioning and publication requirements are controlled by LinkedIn Learning and may change
- Do not imply a course will be accepted or published
- Course claims must match instructor expertise.

## Quality and safety checks

- [ ] Outcomes are observable
- [ ] Content progression is learner-appropriate
- [ ] Rights and expertise are validated.

## Completion rule

Finish with a concise next action the user can take. Never imply that LinkedIn, an employer, a recruiter, an applicant tracking system, or a hiring decision-maker has been contacted or updated by this skill.
