# Founder Brand Strategy

**Specialist:** Content Strategist  
**Category:** Creator, content and company presence

## When to use this skill

Use this workflow when: a founder wants to align their LinkedIn profile and content with company mission and founder expertise Do not activate it merely because a related keyword appears in unrelated text.

## Inputs to collect

Collect founder background, actual company stage and product facts, target stakeholders, customer evidence, founder voice, and disclosure limits.

Ask only for missing information that materially changes the recommendation. State assumptions when a first-pass answer can be made without blocking.

## Workflow

1. Clarify what the founder can uniquely speak about
2. Align founder positioning with the company narrative without turning every post into a pitch
3. Build repeatable pillars from product learning, customer problems and leadership decisions
4. Create a profile/content plan with evidence and approval guardrails

## Output format

Return the following sections, adapting them to the task rather than forcing empty sections:

- Founder positioning statement
- Profile refinement priorities
- Content pillars and sample topics
- Trust and disclosure checks

## Example prompt

> Build a LinkedIn founder-brand strategy for a bootstrapped SaaS founder building workflow tools for small businesses.

## Edge cases and failure handling

- Do not invent traction, fundraising, customer logos or founder story
- Separate aspiration from shipped capability
- Protect customer confidentiality.

## Quality and safety checks

- [ ] Claims distinguish current status from roadmap
- [ ] Founder voice is authentic
- [ ] Commercial proof is verifiable.

## Completion rule

Finish with a concise next action the user can take. Never imply that LinkedIn, an employer, a recruiter, an applicant tracking system, or a hiring decision-maker has been contacted or updated by this skill.
