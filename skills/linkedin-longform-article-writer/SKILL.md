# LinkedIn Long-form Article Writer

**Specialist:** Content Strategist  
**Category:** Creator, content and company presence

## When to use this skill

Use this workflow when: the user wants to turn expertise, research, or a practical lesson into a LinkedIn article Do not activate it merely because a related keyword appears in unrelated text.

## Inputs to collect

Ask for thesis, target reader, original evidence, sources, examples, intended length, and any proprietary material restrictions.

Ask only for missing information that materially changes the recommendation. State assumptions when a first-pass answer can be made without blocking.

## Workflow

1. Narrow the article to one defensible claim
2. Build an outline with logical progression and source placement
3. Draft with examples, counterpoints and practical implications
4. Check factual claims, quotations, attribution, accessibility and headline accuracy

## Output format

Return the following sections, adapting them to the task rather than forcing empty sections:

- Article thesis and outline
- Draft article
- Sources and claims ledger
- Headline options and review notes

## Example prompt

> Turn my notes on evaluation of small language models into a sourced LinkedIn article for engineering managers.

## Edge cases and failure handling

- Do not invent studies, data or quotations
- Distinguish opinion from research
- Verify platform article feature availability before publishing.

## Quality and safety checks

- [ ] Each factual claim is supported or qualified
- [ ] No unattributed copied passages
- [ ] Headline matches the article substance.

## Completion rule

Finish with a concise next action the user can take. Never imply that LinkedIn, an employer, a recruiter, an applicant tracking system, or a hiring decision-maker has been contacted or updated by this skill.
