# Employer Page and Reputation Audit

**Specialist:** Recruiter Operations Partner  
**Category:** Recruiting and talent operations

## When to use this skill

Use this workflow when: an employer brand or talent team wants to audit its LinkedIn company presence and public candidate-facing credibility Do not activate it merely because a related keyword appears in unrelated text.

## Inputs to collect

Request official company Page details, career site, job ads, employee value proposition, public reviews/feedback, and target talent audience.

Ask only for missing information that materially changes the recommendation. State assumptions when a first-pass answer can be made without blocking.

## Workflow

1. Check consistency across company Page, career site and job postings
2. Review role clarity, culture claims, employee evidence and candidate pathways
3. Flag outdated or unsupported statements
4. Prioritize corrections and content that answers candidate questions

## Output format

Return the following sections, adapting them to the task rather than forcing empty sections:

- Employer presence audit
- Consistency and trust gaps
- Content and Page recommendations
- Claims requiring validation

## Example prompt

> Audit our employer brand across LinkedIn Page and careers page for a graduate hiring campaign.

## Edge cases and failure handling

- Do not fabricate employee sentiment or claim representative reviews from a few examples
- Avoid disclosing employee/candidate records
- Feature availability may vary.

## Quality and safety checks

- [ ] Claims match official evidence
- [ ] Feedback sample limits are stated
- [ ] Actions have owners.

## Completion rule

Finish with a concise next action the user can take. Never imply that LinkedIn, an employer, a recruiter, an applicant tracking system, or a hiring decision-maker has been contacted or updated by this skill.
