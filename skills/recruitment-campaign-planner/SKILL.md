# Recruitment Campaign Planner

**Specialist:** Recruiter Operations Partner  
**Category:** Recruiting and talent operations

## When to use this skill

Use this workflow when: a talent team wants to plan a targeted hiring campaign for a defined role or talent segment Do not activate it merely because a related keyword appears in unrelated text.

## Inputs to collect

Collect approved role brief, hiring targets, audience, channels, creative assets, sourcing budget, time window and reporting needs.

Ask only for missing information that materially changes the recommendation. State assumptions when a first-pass answer can be made without blocking.

## Workflow

1. Define audience and job-relevant value proposition
2. Select channels and content formats supported by available resources
3. Prepare campaign assets, approval owners and accessibility checks
4. Track process outcomes and revise on evidence, not impressions alone

## Output format

Return the following sections, adapting them to the task rather than forcing empty sections:

- Campaign brief
- Audience and channel plan
- Asset calendar and ownership
- Outcome measures and review cadence

## Example prompt

> Plan a campus hiring campaign for entry-level cloud engineering roles across three universities.

## Edge cases and failure handling

- Do not target protected groups using inferred traits
- Campaign access and spend need owner approval
- Reach is not equivalent to qualified applicants.

## Quality and safety checks

- [ ] Campaign criteria are tied to role and channel
- [ ] Assets are truthful and accessible
- [ ] Metrics reflect quality and process outcomes.

## Completion rule

Finish with a concise next action the user can take. Never imply that LinkedIn, an employer, a recruiter, an applicant tracking system, or a hiring decision-maker has been contacted or updated by this skill.
