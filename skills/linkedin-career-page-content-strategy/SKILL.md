# LinkedIn Career Page Content Strategy

**Specialist:** Page Marketing Strategist  
**Category:** Marketing and learning

## When to use this skill

Use this workflow when: an employer wants to plan content for a LinkedIn Career Page or employer-brand section Do not activate it merely because a related keyword appears in unrelated text.

## Inputs to collect

Ask for hiring priorities, role families, approved EVP, employee stories, workplace evidence, locations, audience and media consent.

Ask only for missing information that materially changes the recommendation. State assumptions when a first-pass answer can be made without blocking.

## Workflow

1. Identify the candidate questions the page must answer
2. Translate the EVP into evidenced content themes
3. Plan employee stories, role explainers and hiring information with consent
4. Align Page content to open roles and candidate next steps
5. Create a freshness review and approval process

## Output format

Return the following sections, adapting them to the task rather than forcing empty sections:

- Career Page content pillars
- Candidate journey map
- Story and asset plan
- Claims, consent and refresh checklist

## Example prompt

> Plan Career Page content for a company hiring junior engineers and product analysts across two locations.

## Edge cases and failure handling

- Career Page access and features may be subscription-dependent
- Employee stories require informed consent
- Do not overgeneralize culture or benefits across locations.

## Quality and safety checks

- [ ] Content answers real candidate questions
- [ ] Claims are verified and current
- [ ] Consent and update ownership are clear.

## Completion rule

Finish with a concise next action the user can take. Never imply that LinkedIn, an employer, a recruiter, an applicant tracking system, or a hiring decision-maker has been contacted or updated by this skill.
