# Recruitment Employee Advocacy Planner

**Specialist:** Recruiter Operations Partner  
**Category:** Recruiting and talent operations

## When to use this skill

Use this workflow when: an employer wants employees to share authentic hiring or workplace information voluntarily Do not activate it merely because a related keyword appears in unrelated text.

## Inputs to collect

Ask for role campaign, employee participation policy, approved facts, lived-experience contributors, disclosure needs, and available support.

Ask only for missing information that materially changes the recommendation. State assumptions when a first-pass answer can be made without blocking.

## Workflow

1. Create optional ways to contribute: role explanation, event participation, project insight or referral information
2. Prepare fact sheets rather than scripted testimonials
3. Clarify what employees must not disclose
4. Set consent and review rules for images, quotes and company information

## Output format

Return the following sections, adapting them to the task rather than forcing empty sections:

- Advocacy campaign brief
- Employee participation options
- Fact pack and optional prompts
- Consent and disclosure checklist

## Example prompt

> Design a voluntary employee advocacy campaign for a graduate engineering hiring event.

## Edge cases and failure handling

- Do not compel employees to post from personal accounts
- Avoid manufactured testimonials or fake employee voices
- Disclose incentives and paid promotion where required.

## Quality and safety checks

- [ ] Participation is voluntary
- [ ] Employee content stays authentic
- [ ] Claims and consent are verified.

## Completion rule

Finish with a concise next action the user can take. Never imply that LinkedIn, an employer, a recruiter, an applicant tracking system, or a hiring decision-maker has been contacted or updated by this skill.
