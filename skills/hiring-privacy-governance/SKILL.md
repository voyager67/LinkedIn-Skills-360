# Hiring Data Privacy and Retention Review

**Specialist:** Recruiter Operations Partner  
**Category:** Recruiting and talent operations

## When to use this skill

Use this workflow when: a recruiting team wants to review what candidate data it collects, accesses, stores, shares, or retains Do not activate it merely because a related keyword appears in unrelated text.

## Inputs to collect

Ask for process map, data types, collection points, systems, access roles, retention rules, vendors and applicable jurisdiction (if known).

Ask only for missing information that materially changes the recommendation. State assumptions when a first-pass answer can be made without blocking.

## Workflow

1. Inventory candidate data by stage and purpose
2. Identify unnecessary collection, broad access, uncontrolled exports and unapproved model use
3. Map retention/deletion responsibilities and incident escalation
4. Produce policy questions for qualified privacy/legal owners

## Output format

Return the following sections, adapting them to the task rather than forcing empty sections:

- Candidate-data inventory
- Access and purpose map
- Risk and minimization recommendations
- Policy owner questions

## Example prompt

> Review our candidate data workflow for unnecessary collection and unclear retention responsibilities.

## Edge cases and failure handling

- Do not claim legal compliance or provide jurisdiction-specific legal conclusions
- Never paste sensitive candidate data into an unapproved service
- Escalate legal uncertainty.

## Quality and safety checks

- [ ] Data purposes are specific
- [ ] Minimization is prioritized
- [ ] Legal/privacy review is clearly required where relevant.

## Completion rule

Finish with a concise next action the user can take. Never imply that LinkedIn, an employer, a recruiter, an applicant tracking system, or a hiring decision-maker has been contacted or updated by this skill.
