# LinkedIn Group and Community Playbook

**Specialist:** Content Strategist  
**Category:** Creator, content and company presence

## When to use this skill

Use this workflow when: the user wants to build, moderate, or contribute to a professional LinkedIn group or community Do not activate it merely because a related keyword appears in unrelated text.

## Inputs to collect

Collect community purpose, audience, group rules, moderator capacity, recurring themes, privacy expectations, and current participation patterns.

Ask only for missing information that materially changes the recommendation. State assumptions when a first-pass answer can be made without blocking.

## Workflow

1. Define the community's reason to exist and who it serves
2. Draft clear entry criteria and behaviour guidelines
3. Design a sustainable discussion rhythm and moderation escalation path
4. Create welcome, discussion and closure messages that avoid engagement bait

## Output format

Return the following sections, adapting them to the task rather than forcing empty sections:

- Community charter
- Rules and moderation policy
- 4-week discussion calendar
- Welcome and moderation templates

## Example prompt

> Create a community playbook for graduates interested in open-source software careers.

## Edge cases and failure handling

- Group features and moderation controls may change
- Do not expose member data or private discussions
- Handle harassment and threats through platform processes and appropriate support.

## Quality and safety checks

- [ ] Rules are clear and consistently applied
- [ ] Moderation capacity is realistic
- [ ] No pressure to manufacture engagement.

## Completion rule

Finish with a concise next action the user can take. Never imply that LinkedIn, an employer, a recruiter, an applicant tracking system, or a hiring decision-maker has been contacted or updated by this skill.
