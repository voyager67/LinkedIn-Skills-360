# Community Comment and Reputation Response

**Specialist:** Content Strategist  
**Category:** Creator, content and company presence

## When to use this skill

Use this workflow when: a professional or Page owner wants to respond to criticism, misinformation, conflict, or a sensitive public comment on LinkedIn Do not activate it merely because a related keyword appears in unrelated text.

## Inputs to collect

Request the exact comment/thread with sensitive data redacted, context, relevant verified facts, company policy, desired outcome, and approval owner.

Ask only for missing information that materially changes the recommendation. State assumptions when a first-pass answer can be made without blocking.

## Workflow

1. Classify the issue: question, complaint, factual dispute, abuse, safety risk, or legal concern
2. Verify the facts and decide whether public response is appropriate
3. Draft a calm, specific acknowledgement and next step
4. Escalate privacy, security, safety, legal or personnel issues to accountable channels

## Output format

Return the following sections, adapting them to the task rather than forcing empty sections:

- Issue classification and risk level
- Proposed public reply or no-reply rationale
- Escalation owner and response plan
- Facts requiring verification

## Example prompt

> Draft a professional response to this customer complaint without disclosing private account information.

## Edge cases and failure handling

- Do not disclose customer, employee or candidate details
- Threats or safety issues require appropriate escalation
- Do not argue or fabricate a resolution.

## Quality and safety checks

- [ ] Response acknowledges only verified facts
- [ ] No private data is revealed
- [ ] Escalation is recommended when needed.

## Completion rule

Finish with a concise next action the user can take. Never imply that LinkedIn, an employer, a recruiter, an applicant tracking system, or a hiring decision-maker has been contacted or updated by this skill.
