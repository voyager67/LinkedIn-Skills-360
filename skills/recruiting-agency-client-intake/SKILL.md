# Recruitment Agency Client Intake

**Specialist:** Recruiter Operations Partner  
**Category:** Recruiting and talent operations

## When to use this skill

Use this workflow when: an agency recruiter wants to qualify a new client brief and agree search delivery expectations Do not activate it merely because a related keyword appears in unrelated text.

## Inputs to collect

Collect role brief, hiring motivation, exclusivity, fee terms, client decision process, candidate ownership rules, timeline, market constraints and communication cadence.

Ask only for missing information that materially changes the recommendation. State assumptions when a first-pass answer can be made without blocking.

## Workflow

1. Clarify the client problem and hiring urgency
2. Validate role details, compensation, location and decision path
3. Agree candidate profile, evidence criteria and feedback SLAs
4. Document commercial and data-handling terms for human/legal review

## Output format

Return the following sections, adapting them to the task rather than forcing empty sections:

- Client intake summary
- Search mandate and success definition
- Client questions and unresolved terms
- Delivery and communication plan

## Example prompt

> Prepare an intake checklist for an agency taking on a confidential senior engineering search.

## Edge cases and failure handling

- Do not draft binding legal terms as advice
- Do not assume exclusivity or candidate ownership
- Protect client and candidate confidential data.

## Quality and safety checks

- [ ] Mandate and commercial assumptions are explicit
- [ ] Search criteria are agreed
- [ ] Legal review is flagged where needed.

## Completion rule

Finish with a concise next action the user can take. Never imply that LinkedIn, an employer, a recruiter, an applicant tracking system, or a hiring decision-maker has been contacted or updated by this skill.
