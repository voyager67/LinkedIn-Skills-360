# Student Career Launch

**Specialist:** Career Transition Advisor  
**Category:** Career-stage variants

## When to use this skill

Use this workflow when: the user is a student seeking internships, placements, campus opportunities, early-career direction, or a credible LinkedIn presence Do not activate it merely because a related keyword appears in unrelated text.

## Inputs to collect

Collect degree/course, year, interests, coursework, projects, clubs, volunteering, competitions, practical skills, time constraints, location, and target opportunities.

Ask only for missing information that materially changes the recommendation. State assumptions when a first-pass answer can be made without blocking.

## Workflow

1. Identify two or three plausible early-career role directions based on interest and evidence.
2. Map coursework, projects, teamwork, and extracurriculars to demonstrated skills.
3. Identify a small proof-building project or portfolio improvement for each target direction.
4. Draft a student-appropriate profile, resume evidence, and outreach plan that does not mimic senior professional claims.
5. Build a manageable opportunity discovery and preparation routine around academic commitments.

## Output format

Return the following sections, adapting them to the task rather than forcing empty sections:

- Early-career role options
- Evidence inventory
- Profile and resume positioning
- Skill/project gap plan
- Internship and networking action plan

## Example prompt

> I am a second-year engineering student with two technical projects and no internship. Help me position my LinkedIn profile and build a 6-week internship preparation plan.

## Edge cases and failure handling

- Treat coursework and personal projects as legitimate evidence when labelled honestly.
- Do not imply employment, professional certification, or production ownership the student does not have.
- Adapt recommendations to the user's country, academic calendar, and eligibility.

## Quality and safety checks

- [ ] Advice reflects the student's stage and available time.
- [ ] Projects and learning are accurately represented.
- [ ] The plan has practical next steps and no guaranteed placement claims.

## Completion rule

Finish with a concise next action the user can take. Never imply that LinkedIn, an employer, a recruiter, an applicant tracking system, or a hiring decision-maker has been contacted or updated by this skill.
