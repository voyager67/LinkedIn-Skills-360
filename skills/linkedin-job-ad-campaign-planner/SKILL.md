# LinkedIn Job Ad Campaign Planner

**Specialist:** LinkedIn Ads Strategist  
**Category:** Marketing and learning

## When to use this skill

Use this workflow when: an employer wants to plan paid promotion for a LinkedIn job or talent campaign Do not activate it merely because a related keyword appears in unrelated text.

## Inputs to collect

Collect approved job description, target talent profile, must-have role facts, geography, budget, campaign dates, application destination, and hiring funnel goals.

Ask only for missing information that materially changes the recommendation. State assumptions when a first-pass answer can be made without blocking.

## Workflow

1. Check the job is clear and approved before promotion
2. Define a job-related target audience and exclude inappropriate attributes
3. Choose a campaign goal and application path that the employer can support
4. Build a budget, creative and candidate response plan
5. Define applicant quality and process measures, not just clicks

## Output format

Return the following sections, adapting them to the task rather than forcing empty sections:

- Hiring campaign brief
- Role and audience rationale
- Creative and destination plan
- Budget assumptions and process metrics

## Example prompt

> Plan a LinkedIn job promotion campaign for graduate software engineers. Use only confirmed role details and keep all selection decisions human-led.

## Edge cases and failure handling

- Paid job objectives and talent lead access may require specific contracts or permissions
- Do not target protected traits
- Do not equate applicant volume with candidate quality.

## Quality and safety checks

- [ ] Job details are accurate and approved
- [ ] Targeting is job-related
- [ ] Candidate follow-up capacity is considered.

## Completion rule

Finish with a concise next action the user can take. Never imply that LinkedIn, an employer, a recruiter, an applicant tracking system, or a hiring decision-maker has been contacted or updated by this skill.
