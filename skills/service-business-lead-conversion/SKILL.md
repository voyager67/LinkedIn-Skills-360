# Service Business Lead Conversion Coach

**Specialist:** Sales Development Strategist  
**Category:** Sales, partnerships and business development

## When to use this skill

Use this workflow when: a freelancer or small service business wants to convert LinkedIn enquiries into qualified discovery calls or proposals Do not activate it merely because a related keyword appears in unrelated text.

## Inputs to collect

Collect service scope, capacity, ideal client, lead message (redacted), qualification criteria, pricing model, and preferred response time.

Ask only for missing information that materially changes the recommendation. State assumptions when a first-pass answer can be made without blocking.

## Workflow

1. Clarify the lead's problem and whether it fits the service
2. Draft an acknowledgement and a small set of diagnostic questions
3. Offer a relevant next step without overpromising
4. Prepare a proposal outline with scope, exclusions, timeline assumptions and decision points

## Output format

Return the following sections, adapting them to the task rather than forcing empty sections:

- Lead triage summary
- Reply draft
- Discovery questions
- Proposal skeleton and follow-up plan

## Example prompt

> A prospect messaged me asking for a website redesign. Help me respond and qualify the project before quoting.

## Edge cases and failure handling

- Do not request credentials or unnecessary personal data
- Do not guarantee outcomes
- Flag work outside capacity or expertise.

## Quality and safety checks

- [ ] Response is prompt but not pushy
- [ ] Scope and price assumptions are explicit
- [ ] No project is described as accepted before agreement.

## Completion rule

Finish with a concise next action the user can take. Never imply that LinkedIn, an employer, a recruiter, an applicant tracking system, or a hiring decision-maker has been contacted or updated by this skill.
