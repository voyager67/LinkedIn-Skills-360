# Sales Meeting Follow-up and Mutual Action Plan

**Specialist:** Sales Development Strategist  
**Category:** Sales, partnerships and business development

## When to use this skill

Use this workflow when: the user wants to turn a real LinkedIn-originated sales conversation into clear next steps Do not activate it merely because a related keyword appears in unrelated text.

## Inputs to collect

Collect meeting notes with sensitive details redacted, agreed pains, participants and roles, explicit commitments, timeline, and open questions.

Ask only for missing information that materially changes the recommendation. State assumptions when a first-pass answer can be made without blocking.

## Workflow

1. Separate what the prospect said from seller interpretation
2. Summarize needs, constraints and agreed outcomes
3. Create action items with owner and due date only when agreed
4. Draft a concise recap and mutual next-step plan for review

## Output format

Return the following sections, adapting them to the task rather than forcing empty sections:

- Fact-checked meeting summary
- Agreed action register
- Open questions and risks
- Follow-up message

## Example prompt

> Turn these discovery call notes into a clear follow-up with agreed actions; do not add promises we did not make.

## Edge cases and failure handling

- Do not circulate confidential notes beyond intended recipients
- Never convert a tentative idea into a commitment
- Verify dates and owners before sending.

## Quality and safety checks

- [ ] Every commitment is evidenced by the notes
- [ ] Owners and dates are confirmed
- [ ] Message is reviewed by the user.

## Completion rule

Finish with a concise next action the user can take. Never imply that LinkedIn, an employer, a recruiter, an applicant tracking system, or a hiring decision-maker has been contacted or updated by this skill.
