# Inclusive and Accessible Hiring Review

**Specialist:** Recruiter Operations Partner  
**Category:** Recruiting and talent operations

## When to use this skill

Use this workflow when: a hiring team wants to review job ads, interview stages, or assessments for unnecessary barriers and accessibility Do not activate it merely because a related keyword appears in unrelated text.

## Inputs to collect

Collect role criteria, job ad, process steps, assessment formats, candidate instructions, accommodation pathways, and target jurisdiction/policy context.

Ask only for missing information that materially changes the recommendation. State assumptions when a first-pass answer can be made without blocking.

## Workflow

1. Check criteria against actual role tasks and remove unnecessary proxies
2. Review plain language, essential information, scheduling and assessment access
3. Identify ways to provide consistent instructions and reasonable flexibility
4. Flag decisions that require HR, legal or accessibility expertise

## Output format

Return the following sections, adapting them to the task rather than forcing empty sections:

- Barrier review
- Recommended inclusive wording
- Process accessibility checklist
- Escalation points

## Example prompt

> Review this graduate developer job description and interview loop for avoidable barriers.

## Edge cases and failure handling

- Do not infer disability or protected status
- Do not promise legal compliance
- Accommodations must follow approved confidential processes.

## Quality and safety checks

- [ ] Changes preserve job-related standards
- [ ] Barriers are specific and observable
- [ ] Human specialists review legal and accommodation questions.

## Completion rule

Finish with a concise next action the user can take. Never imply that LinkedIn, an employer, a recruiter, an applicant tracking system, or a hiring decision-maker has been contacted or updated by this skill.
