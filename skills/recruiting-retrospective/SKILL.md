# Recruiting Retrospective and Continuous Improvement

**Specialist:** Recruiter Operations Partner  
**Category:** Recruiting and talent operations

## When to use this skill

Use this workflow when: a hiring team wants to review a completed hiring campaign and agree improvements Do not activate it merely because a related keyword appears in unrelated text.

## Inputs to collect

Collect process timeline, anonymized funnel metrics, candidate feedback themes, hiring-team observations, goals and any known data limitations.

Ask only for missing information that materially changes the recommendation. State assumptions when a first-pass answer can be made without blocking.

## Workflow

1. Review what was expected versus observed
2. Identify process friction from evidence rather than blame
3. Separate controllable workflow issues from market or role changes
4. Choose a small number of experiments with owners and revisit dates

## Output format

Return the following sections, adapting them to the task rather than forcing empty sections:

- Retrospective summary
- What worked and what did not
- Root-cause hypotheses and confidence
- Improvement actions and measures

## Example prompt

> Run a retrospective for our last campus hiring process using these anonymized figures and feedback themes.

## Edge cases and failure handling

- Do not attribute outcomes to one recruiter or interviewer without evidence
- Small feedback samples are not representative
- Protect candidate confidentiality.

## Quality and safety checks

- [ ] Findings are blameless and evidence-based
- [ ] Actions are owned
- [ ] No causal claim without support.

## Completion rule

Finish with a concise next action the user can take. Never imply that LinkedIn, an employer, a recruiter, an applicant tracking system, or a hiring decision-maker has been contacted or updated by this skill.
