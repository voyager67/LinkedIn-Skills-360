# Hiring Manager Intake Alignment

**Specialist:** Recruiter Operations Partner  
**Category:** Recruiting and talent operations

## When to use this skill

Use this workflow when: a recruiter wants to align a hiring manager and interview team on role scope, criteria, process, and decision ownership Do not activate it merely because a related keyword appears in unrelated text.

## Inputs to collect

Collect the manager's role brief, business outcomes, level, must-have criteria, stakeholders, timeline and areas of disagreement.

Ask only for missing information that materially changes the recommendation. State assumptions when a first-pass answer can be made without blocking.

## Workflow

1. Surface assumptions and resolve competing expectations
2. Translate desired traits into job-related observable behaviours
3. Agree on interview ownership, decision rights and feedback deadlines
4. Record unresolved trade-offs and the approval needed before sourcing starts

## Output format

Return the following sections, adapting them to the task rather than forcing empty sections:

- Alignment agenda
- Agreed role scorecard
- Decision and owner log
- Sourcing-ready confirmation checklist

## Example prompt

> Prepare an intake meeting agenda to align a hiring manager and recruiters on an entry-level analyst vacancy.

## Edge cases and failure handling

- Do not treat personal preferences as essential qualifications
- Escalate compensation or policy conflicts
- No sourcing begins on unapproved assumptions.

## Quality and safety checks

- [ ] Role criteria and decision rights are documented
- [ ] Essential criteria have rationale
- [ ] Open conflicts are visible.

## Completion rule

Finish with a concise next action the user can take. Never imply that LinkedIn, an employer, a recruiter, an applicant tracking system, or a hiring decision-maker has been contacted or updated by this skill.
