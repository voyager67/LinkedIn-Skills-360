# LinkedIn Ads Objective and Budget Planner

**Specialist:** LinkedIn Ads Strategist  
**Category:** Marketing and learning

## When to use this skill

Use this workflow when: the user wants to plan a LinkedIn Campaign Manager campaign objective, funnel role, budget hypothesis and success measures Do not activate it merely because a related keyword appears in unrelated text.

## Inputs to collect

Collect business goal, offer, audience, geography, campaign window, budget range, landing page, available creative, and prior campaign data.

Ask only for missing information that materially changes the recommendation. State assumptions when a first-pass answer can be made without blocking.

## Workflow

1. Translate the business goal into awareness, consideration or conversion intent
2. Choose an objective that matches the desired action rather than an easy vanity metric
3. Map the objective to a plausible format and destination using current official guidance
4. Build a bounded budget and test plan with assumptions, minimum data needs and approval points

## Output format

Return the following sections, adapting them to the task rather than forcing empty sections:

- Campaign brief and funnel stage
- Recommended objective with rationale
- Budget and flighting assumptions
- Test plan and success metrics

## Example prompt

> Plan a small LinkedIn Campaign Manager test for a B2B webinar. Recommend the objective, format, budget assumptions and what must be measured.

## Edge cases and failure handling

- Available objectives, formats and bidding controls can vary by account, market and campaign setup
- Do not promise reach, leads, or conversion cost
- Media spend requires an authorized human approval.

## Quality and safety checks

- [ ] Objective matches the intended outcome
- [ ] Budget assumptions are transparent
- [ ] Success measures include quality, not just volume.

## Completion rule

Finish with a concise next action the user can take. Never imply that LinkedIn, an employer, a recruiter, an applicant tracking system, or a hiring decision-maker has been contacted or updated by this skill.
