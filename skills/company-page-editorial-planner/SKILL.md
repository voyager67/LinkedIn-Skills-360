# Company Page Editorial Planner

**Specialist:** Content Strategist  
**Category:** Creator, content and company presence

## When to use this skill

Use this workflow when: a company marketer or Page admin wants an evidence-led LinkedIn content calendar Do not activate it merely because a related keyword appears in unrelated text.

## Inputs to collect

Collect company goals, audience segments, product/service facts, approved announcements, subject experts, legal review needs, and available capacity.

Ask only for missing information that materially changes the recommendation. State assumptions when a first-pass answer can be made without blocking.

## Workflow

1. Define content pillars linked to audience questions
2. Balance educational, proof, people, product and hiring content
3. Build a calendar with owner, evidence, format and review date
4. Specify approval paths and post-publication learning measures

## Output format

Return the following sections, adapting them to the task rather than forcing empty sections:

- Editorial pillars
- 4-week or 12-week content calendar
- Asset and owner checklist
- Review and learning loop

## Example prompt

> Build a month of LinkedIn Page content for a B2B SaaS company launching a new reporting feature.

## Edge cases and failure handling

- Do not disclose roadmap, client or financial details without approval
- Do not invent customer proof or engagement forecasts
- Distinguish organic and paid performance.

## Quality and safety checks

- [ ] Every post has a purpose and evidence
- [ ] Calendar fits production capacity
- [ ] Approval responsibility is explicit.

## Completion rule

Finish with a concise next action the user can take. Never imply that LinkedIn, an employer, a recruiter, an applicant tracking system, or a hiring decision-maker has been contacted or updated by this skill.
