# Profile review rubric

Use this rubric as a transparent coaching aid, not as LinkedIn's ranking formula or an employer's assessment.

| Dimension | Review question | Strong evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Goal clarity | Can a relevant reader understand the profile's direction quickly? | Clear target role, audience, or professional focus |
| Audience relevance | Does the profile speak to the intended reader? | Relevant needs and terminology without keyword stuffing |
| Headline | Does it communicate focus and evidence? | Specific role/skill/value statement supported by experience |
| About opening | Does the opening make the profile's purpose clear? | Short, natural positioning statement |
| About body | Is the narrative credible and useful? | Relevant background, strengths, proof, and next step |
| Experience | Are contributions understandable? | Specific actions, scope, and outcomes with accurate attribution |
| Skills | Are listed skills relevant and demonstrated? | Evidence-backed, current, prioritized skills |
| Projects/portfolio | Can a reader inspect proof? | Clear project summaries, links, demos, or artifacts |
| Featured section | Does it surface useful evidence? | Selected work, portfolio, writing, or contact path when relevant |
| Consistency | Do sections support the same direction? | Coherent titles, dates, and focus |
| Readability | Is it easy to scan and understand? | Plain language, useful structure, low jargon |
| Trust and completeness | Are details accurate and current? | No unsupported claims; current links and role details |

Suggested scoring: rate each dimension 0–4 (0 = absent or misleading, 1 = weak, 2 = partial, 3 = solid, 4 = strong), then convert to a percentage only if a numeric summary is useful. Show the individual ratings and caveat that the rubric is subjective coaching feedback, not an external score.
