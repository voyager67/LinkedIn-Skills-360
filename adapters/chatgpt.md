# ChatGPT

The most portable approach is to paste or attach the chosen `SKILL.md` in a conversation, then provide the inputs requested by that skill. You can also keep frequently used workflows in a supported project or instruction surface available in your ChatGPT workspace.

For a multi-step task, provide `agents/orchestrator.md` plus the primary specialist prompt. If the environment does not provide separate agent delegation, ask the model to follow the routing steps sequentially.

Do not assume that sharing a GitHub repository URL installs the skills or makes them persistently available. Avoid uploading full resumes or candidate records when a redacted excerpt is sufficient.
